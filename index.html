Why IoT Security Matters

The Internet of Things (IoT) connects billions of devices (count, no unit)—smart thermostats, industrial sensors, medical devices, vehicles—to the internet [1]. Each connection is a potential entry point for attackers.

As software developers, you will build, integrate, or maintain systems that interact with IoT devices. Understanding the unique security challenges of these devices is essential, even if you don't work on embedded firmware directly.

What Makes IoT Different

  • Resource constraints: Limited CPU, memory, and power make traditional security (TLS, heavy crypto) difficult.
  • Long lifecycles: Devices may operate for 10+ years (unit: years) without updates.
  • Physical exposure: Attackers often have physical access to devices.
  • Heterogeneous ecosystems: Multiple vendors, protocols, and update mechanisms.
  • Scale: A single vulnerability can affect millions of devices (count, no unit) simultaneously.
